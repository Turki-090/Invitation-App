# Privacy and Saudi PDPL readiness

Names, phone numbers, RSVP decisions, attendance, and event-location associations are personal data. The platform minimizes collection to invitation operations, documents purpose and retention, limits access through event permissions, and records sensitive administrative actions.

Before production launch, the team must document the physical processing locations and subprocessors for the database, backups, logs, analytics, messaging, email, error monitoring, and object storage. International transfer and retention decisions require legal and operational review for the selected Saudi/GCC deployment.

Required operational capabilities include access/export handling, deletion and recovery workflows, vendor inventory, incident response, backup restore tests, and production-access logging. Analytics must use internal IDs and product events, never guest names, phone numbers, or invitation tokens.
